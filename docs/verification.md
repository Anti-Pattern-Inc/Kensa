## First-run checklist

Count the following in a real environment to judge whether it is "ready to use" (on 2026-11-02, items 2, 3, 5 and 6 were run with one development account and a test workload. The results are written under each item. Items 1 and 4 have not been done).

1. **Number of wrong ○** — check whether any ○ without grounds appears. Cross-check with OpenItems.
2. **Number of assumed ✗, and the breakdown of missing permissions (`denied`)** — look at the confirmed rate in `status`. *(Done: of the 37 checks, `ok` 35, `not_enabled` 2, `denied` 0. The Macie misclassification was fixed. Only 3 items were judged from scan evidence; the remaining 304 are assumed)*
3. **Gaps and duplicates** — check that every `choice_id` of the lens has a status and that there are no duplicates (compare the number of `items` in `export --format json` with the total number of choices in the W-A Tool). *(Done: 307 items, 0 duplicates, 0 without a verdict. But the items were fetched from the W-A Tool, so this only compares against the same source. We have not cross-checked against the official documentation)*
4. **Number of questions and time taken** — check that the decision cards (at most 30) and the 9 business questions are not too many. "Too many questions" and "not enough business hearing" are still **hypotheses**, to be tested with 1 or 2 real users.
5. **`push` dry-run → `--apply`** — the behavior of `ChoiceUpdates`, the handling of "None of these", and `--milestone`. Whether the warning about assumed items is as expected. *(Done: as in the table above. The warning about assumed items also appeared. Note that sending assumed items too turns every question into "answered", so "unanswered 0" in `precheck` no longer reflects the real state)*
6. **Re-run** — with `snapshot` → rescan → `diff`, check that people's answers are kept and only the regressions appear. *(Done: after `snapshot`, we ran `apply` with a worse piece of evidence, and `diff` showed only "worse 1 (○→△)". Whether people's answers are kept across a rescan has not been tried on a real account)*
